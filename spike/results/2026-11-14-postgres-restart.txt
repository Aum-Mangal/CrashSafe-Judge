Real Postgres restart under a running worker (DECISIONS.md, 93)

Setup: dev distro, fresh database demo_restart, one worker with POLL_SECONDS=60, so only a
notification can make it claim quickly. `docker compose restart postgres` 3 s after start,
then a job inserted and NOTIFY job_new sent once the worker logged that it was listening again.

postgres container restarted in 0.6 s
worker listening again 1.6 s after the restart began
notified job claimed after 0.05 s (poll interval is 60 s)
job ended completed with verdict OK
worker process still alive: True
